# Parked ideas

Things deliberately left out of the five build phases. Nothing here is a
commitment — this file exists so an idea can be written down and dropped
instead of quietly widening the current phase.

## Nutrition
- Barcode scanning for packaged foods.
- Restaurant menu presets (the plan leans on zinger, shawarma and burgers —
  their published calories could be stored once instead of re-estimated).
- Per-food "last eaten" ordering in pickers, so the common foods float up.
- Water intake.

## Training
- Per-exercise weight increment (2.5 kg suits upper body; 5 kg often suits
  legs). Currently one constant in src/lib/progression.ts.
- Deload weeks.
- Warm-up set calculator.
- Exercise substitution when equipment is taken.
- Volume per muscle group over time.

## Adherence
- Configurable streak threshold (currently 80 in src/lib/adherence.ts).
- Reasons attached to a specific day rather than to the week.
- Correlation between the weekly review reasons and the score.

## App
- Widgets / Live Activity for the rest timer.
- Apple Health / Google Fit weight sync.
- Multiple weight readings per day (the schema keeps one row per date).
- Photo attached to a meal log.
